Executive summary

What to sell in Singapore during haze season

Haze is active right now: 24-hr PSI hit 208–212 in the west on 14–15 Oct 2026 — the season's high — with NEA forecasting Unhealthy to Low Very Unhealthy for 16 Oct. Retailers report air-purifier sales up 76%–400% month-on-month. The demand window is open but short and repeatable; the winning plays are consumables and verification devices (filters, PM2.5 monitors, sealing kits), not the hardware everyone else is already restocking weekly.

Why demand is sticky, and where it caps out

Tailwinds

  • El Niño + positive IOD forecast by MSS/NEA (29 May 2026) → drier Sumatra/Kalimantan, elevated transboundary haze risk; 2015-class analogue.
  • Dense Kalimantan haze drifting in under southeasterly winds; dry conditions persist over southern Sumatra (NEA, 14 Oct).
  • PSI > 300 trigger: MOE will consider closing schools & postponing exams (Desmond Lee, 10 Oct) → step-change demand spike for indoor products.
  • Outdoor workers & businesses already hit (ST); parents cutting outdoor activity → indoor-economy spend.
  • Shoppers now ask about CADR, PM2.5 filtration, filter lifespan (Courts via Zaobao) → informed buyers, upsell works.

Caps & suppressors

  • Free N95s to Pioneer/Merdeka/CHAS/ComCare cardholders, pregnant women, children 7–16, asthma sufferers — via CCs + vending machines at transport hubs (go.gov.sg/hazemask). Removes the most panicked segment from retail.
  • Haze Task Force guarantees replenishment: "sufficient stocks in warehouses and government stockpiles" (NEA 14 Oct) → no scarcity premium on masks.
  • Big-box response is fast: Gain City 100+ models, weekly restocking; Courts says stock sufficient across 12 stores.
  • Spells can end in days — 1 Oct was Moderate (PSI 60–74). Demand-cliff risk is real.

Tier 3 — Services & niche (if you have ops capacity)

  • Purifier rental (daily/weekly, refurbished units): short spells, students, expats; low capex, recurring revenue.
  • Post-haze deep cleaning (dust, ducts, curtains): demand lags season end — schedule now, deliver Nov–Dec.
  • Indoor kids activity kits / delivery-food positioning: live under the PSI>300 school-closure scenario; parents already cutting outdoor activity.

Avoid

  • Standalone N95 / surgical mask resale. Masks are medical devices: importing or supplying requires an HSA dealer's licence (GN-02); a firm was fined S$10k for unlicensed mask production. Government free distribution + Haze Task Force stock guarantees + Price Controller crackdown precedent (2020) = no margin, real legal risk.
  • Premium purifiers >S$700 — IQAir, Dyson, Blueair, Sterra own that shelf with showrooms and warranties.
  • No-brand "N95-style" KN95 stock — counterfeit / NIOSH-claim enforcement risk.
  • Humidifiers marketed for haze — they don't remove PM2.5; such claims are CPFTA unfair-practice exposure.

Channel mix

  • Shopee / Lazada: fees ~5–9% incl. payment; haze keywords spike search — list before PSI crosses 200.
  • TikTok Shop: live demos (candle-in-box vs purifier, live monitor readings) convert during haze peaks.
  • Carousell: fastest zero-fee local listing for sealing kits and cabin filters with meet-up install.
  • Logistics: air freight ~S$4–6/kg, 3–7 days — use it; sea freight (2–3 weeks) will miss the spell. Regional air capacity tightens during haze; book early.
  • Tax: GST applies on commercial imports (low-value-goods rules); register once turnover > S$1M.
