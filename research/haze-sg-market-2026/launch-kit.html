Forwarder 101

Never bought from 1688? Still works

A consolidator gives you a China warehouse address. Sellers ship there domestically; the forwarder flies the parcel to your SG door. No Chinese bank card needed.

StepHow
1 · Sign upPick one of Taobao2SG / Ebaoguo / ImagineShip. Email/phone signup → you get a personal member code + warehouse address (e.g. Guangzhou warehouse + your code).
2 · Use their buying agent (easiest)Send the 1688/Taobao product link to their support. They order it for you (~5% service fee); you pay by PayNow/PayPal. No 1688 account, no Alipay needed.
3 · Or order yourselfSet the delivery address to the forwarder's warehouse, put your member code in the address notes. Pay via Alipay (international card can be linked).
4 · Parcel hits warehouseDomestic leg 1–3 days (usually free). Enter the seller's tracking number (运单号) in the forwarder's system to link the parcel to your account.
5 · Pay international legChoose air freight ¥30–55/kg, declare contents honestly, pay by PayNow/PayPal.
6 · Receive4–6 days to your SG door. If CIF > S$400, 9% GST may apply — normal, already priced into your calculator.
Test order first: run S$200–300 through the forwarder to verify speed and handling before the main lot. All three have Chinese-language support.

Hard rules

What gets you banned or fined

Never touch masks. N95/FFP respirators are HSA-regulated medical devices — importing/supplying without a dealer's licence is an offence. Free government distribution kills the margin anyway.
"Compatible with", never brand logos. Third-party filters: no Xiaomi logo in photos/titles, no "original/genuine" wording. CPFTA false-claim exposure + platform IP takedowns.
Real prices, real claims. No fake strikethrough discounts (CCCS price-transparency enforcement); state measured/claimed CADR only from supplier spec sheets.
Parallel import is legal for consumer electronics/filters; budget 9% GST on CIF when commercial imports exceed S$400. Declare honestly — consolidators screen parcels.
Timing kill-switch. Stop all air-freight reorders the day PSI drops below 100 for 3 consecutive days. Filters stay liquid; monitors do not — exit them at cost if the spell breaks.
