# Domain reference

Multivariate forecasting predicts one or more target series while using correlated targets or auxiliary covariates. Past-only covariates end at the forecast origin; past-future covariates are known throughout the requested horizon. Zero-shot means no task-specific weight update, not an absence of pretraining. Point forecasts estimate a central outcome; quantile forecasts expose a predictive distribution. See `../analysis/terminology.md` for the canonical glossary. [source-001, source-003]
