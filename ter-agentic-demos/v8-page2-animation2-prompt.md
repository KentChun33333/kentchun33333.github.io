# V8 Page 2 / Animation 2 — Build Prompt

## Purpose

Build Page 2 and Animation 2 as a six-frame, orchestrator-led investigation. The animation must demonstrate reproducible delegation, evidence convergence and controlled promotion of a provisional hypothesis into a validated STR finding.

## Storyline

1. Orchestrator scopes the investigation and delegates the customer baseline to the Data Analyst.
2. Data Analyst queries the bank-held customer and related-client profile.
3. Data Analyst ranks transactions and discovers Aster Peak FZE.
4. Entity Resolver and External Web Agent resolve Aster Peak and reopen the mismatch test.
5. Evidence QA compares independent source classes and validates the mismatch.
6. Risk + Narrative converts approved findings and open gaps into a human-review STR draft.

## Dynamic dialogue

- Use the stable workforce roster: OR, DI, CT, DA, ER, EW, QA and RN.
- Expand only the current lead agent.
- Reveal `TASK`, `ACTION`, `REASONING SUMMARY`, and `OUTPUT / HANDOFF` word by word.
- Collaborators may be named in the dialogue or small annotations, but must not open competing dialogues.
- Every non-terminal output must identify the exact next active agent and task.

## Main-canvas behavior

- Retain previous outcomes as compact chips.
- Show only current-frame planning, queries, comparison, validation or finding components.
- Keep the workflow state, current owner and queued state synchronized.
- When Aster Peak’s machinery activity is found, visibly loop back to the mismatch test rather than jumping directly to a risk.
- Evidence QA must complete validation before Risk + Narrative becomes active.

## Investigation controls

- Value alone is not suspicion.
- Distinguish bank-held facts, uploaded documents and simulated web evidence.
- Keep Aster Peak identity and NOB resolution traceable.
- Preserve counterparty #2 as an unresolved human-directed follow-up rather than automatically expanding scope.

## Acceptance checks

- Six frames follow `OR → DA → DA → ER/EW → QA → RN`.
- Frame 1 delegates to Data Analyst, not a phantom KYC/Profile agent.
- Frame 3 hands the discovered entity to Entity Resolver.
- Frame 4 hands the reopened mismatch test to Evidence QA.
- Frame 5 hands the supported mismatch to Risk + Narrative.
- Frame 6 ends at human review and does not imply another autonomous frame.
