# V8 Page 2 / Animation 2 — Presenter and QA Notes

## Presenter storyline

Page 2 shows why orchestration matters: the agents do not merely run in parallel. Each discovery changes the next test, and no risk is promoted until independent evidence converges.

## Talk points by frame

### Frame 1 — Scope

- The Orchestrator opens H1 as provisional and assigns the internal baseline to the Data Analyst.
- Highlight reproducibility: baseline, transactions, mismatch, validation, finding.

### Frame 2 — Customer baseline

- Bank-held NOB and expected scale become the comparison baseline.
- Broad product detail is a limitation, not suspicion.

### Frame 3 — Transaction discovery

- Aster Peak is prioritized because it is the largest debit and 6.7× expected monthly activity.
- Value triggers follow-up; it does not prove the finding.

### Frame 4 — Entity resolution and loop-back

- Exact entity and jurisdiction resolution enables simulated NOB research.
- Machinery activity reopens the earlier document/customer mismatch test.

### Frame 5 — Independent validation

- KYC, invoice, bill of lading, customs, registry and payment evidence are compared explicitly.
- Evidence QA separates nominal narrative from converged evidence.

### Frame 6 — Finding formation

- Risk + Narrative uses only validated claims.
- Counterparty #2 remains queued for a human-directed extension on Page 3.

## Frame-alignment checklist

- Current agent and main-canvas owner match.
- Handoff and next delegation match the next frame.
- Business-stage progress does not mark work complete early.
- The loop-back is visible in both workflow state and main-canvas explanation.
- QA precedes risk formation.
- Final output explicitly requires human review.
