Buy a Condo in Singapore

2021 - 6 - 28
| Design Life
| Reading

In short, my wife and I become PR in Singapore at May 2021. We then decide to buy a house here for a more stable life. After considerations, we bought The Woodleight Residence. The location is near the cental of Singapore, Google Map . There is also a 3D Demo for take a virtual look of it.

Why Woodleight Residence?

First, the general critials for us are listed below.

Item Description
Locations The distance to key facilities like vivo-city, stadium and orchard. The surrounding should be good. Less than 5 mins to MRT
Project A good project that is on top of the MRT, which is more important to be freehold vs 99-lease hold. The project size also important that 665 units are a decent number. We also want new house that it should top from 2018 onward.
Unit 2 Bath Room. High Floor. Larget that 700 sqrt. Price is below 1.5 million SGD

Price vs Locations

  • Note that 130 equals to 1.3 million SGD

Lots of projects are considered, like Park colonial, great sengang residence, poiz residence and …etc. Eventually, base on the supply and market, we decided to upgrade a bit on our budgets and go for a 2 bed unit with lake view at woodleight residence. The top is targeted around SEP 2023. We are looking forward to it.
