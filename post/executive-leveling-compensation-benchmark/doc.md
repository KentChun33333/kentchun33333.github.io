## 2. Singapore Banking & Civil Service Compensation Windows (Post-2025/2026 Verification)

### 2.1 OCBC AVP vs. UOB AVP / VP Segment Parity
Current 2025/2026 employee-reported aggregates and post-June-2025 submissions support these verified institutional ladders:
- **OCBC Career Ladder:** `Officer -> AM -> Manager -> AVP -> VP -> ED -> MD -> MD1 -> GMD`
- **UOB Career Ladder:** `Officer -> AM -> Manager -> AVP1 -> AVP2 -> VP1 -> VP2 -> FVP -> SVP -> ED -> MD`
- **Compensation Windows:**
  - **OCBC:** Assistant Manager **S$48k–66k**, Manager **S$60k–96k**, AVP **S$94k–143k**, VP **S$154k–237k**, ED **S$260k–380k**, MD **S$350k–520k+**, MD1 **S$450k–700k+ / S$650k–1.2M+**, GMD **S$850k–1.4M+ / S$2.8M–4.5M+**.
  - **UOB:** Assistant Manager **S$55k–75k**, Manager **S$55k–77k**, AVP1 **S$82k–109k**, AVP2/VP1 **S$105k–150k**, VP2 **S$145k–174k**, FVP **S$174k–240k**, SVP **S$260k–380k**, ED **S$350k–500k+ / S$450k–700k+**, MD **S$450k–700k+ / S$650k–1.2M+**, Senior MD / ExCo **S$850k–1.4M+ / S$2.8M–4.5M+**.
- **Cross-Institutional Salary & Scope Mapping (ref-ocbc-uob.md):**
  - **TSMC DL / Operator** = OCBC Officer ↔ UOB Officer / Analyst
  - **TSMC G30-31** = OCBC Assistant Manager (AM) ↔ UOB Assistant Manager (AM)
  - **TSMC G31-32** = OCBC Manager ↔ UOB Manager
  - **TSMC G32-34** = OCBC AVP (G33 center; G34 people-manager edge) ↔ UOB [AVP1 -> AVP2 -> VP1 -> VP2]
  - **TSMC G35/36** = OCBC VP -> UOB FVP
  - **TSMC G37/38** = OCBC ED -> UOB SVP
  - **TSMC G39/40** = OCBC MD -> UOB ED
  - **TSMC 40+ (VP, SVP, COO, CTO)** = OCBC GMD (COO, CTO) -> UOB MD (Senior MD, COO, CTO)
- Sources: [OCBC salary aggregate](https://static.glassdoor.sg/Salary/OCBC-Salaries-E9773.htm), [UOB salary aggregate](https://static.glassdoor.sg/Salary/United-Overseas-Bank-Salaries-E11462.htm), [UOB FVP aggregate](https://static.glassdoor.sg/Salary/United-Overseas-Bank-Salaries-E11462_P2.htm), and [UOB published employee-grade table](https://www.uobgroup.com/investor-relations/assets/pdfs/investor/annual/Indonesia/UOBIndo_AR21.pdf).

### 2.2 OCBC VP vs. Singapore Civil Service MX9 (Superscale)
- **OCBC Vice President (VP) Structure:**
  - Monthly Base Salary: **S$11,500 – S$16,500+/month** (Median: ~S$13,500 – S$15,000/mo).
  - Annual Bonus: 3.0 to 5.5 months in corporate, risk, technology, and wealth units.
  - **Annual Total Compensation (TC): S$200,000 – S$300,000 SGD**.
- **Singapore Civil Service MX9 (Superscale Entry / Director):**
  - Monthly Base: **S$11,500 – S$16,500/month**.
  - Total Annual Bonus (AWS + AVC + Performance Bonus): 3.5 to 5.0 months.
  - **Annual Total Compensation (TC): S$200,000 – S$280,000/300k SGD**.
- **Conclusion:** From an annual cash-flow perspective, **OCBC VP (S$200k–S$300k) overlaps SG Gov MX9 (Superscale entry)** and the UOB FVP-to-entry-SVP pay window. In the responsibility-based TSMC segment tree, its core anchor is **TSMC G35/36 = OCBC VP -> UOB FVP**; VP2 is the lower transition edge and SVP the upper department-manager boundary.

> **兩種比較維度的關鍵分流（學歷入口 vs. 成熟職位責任）：**

> **結論：** 每一職級皆有清晰對應對標。台積電 34–40 明確綁定管理階層，而 ASML JG8–11 採技術 IC 與管理雙軌。銀行端（OCBC / UOB）VP 多為高階技術主力 IC 或初中階業務領隊，在層級與責任上等同 TSMC 35（經理）或 36（部經理）。

## 6. Synthesis: How to Interpret Cross-System Benchmark Data

1. **Within Singapore (OCBC, UOB, SG Gov): Use Compensation Windows.**  
   A VP at OCBC making S$200k–S$300k is the financial equivalent of a Civil Service Director entering Superscale (MX9). It reflects what Singapore's market pays for seasoned leadership at age 35–45.
2. **Between Singapore Gov & Taiwan Gov: Use Job Grade & Scope.**  
   Do not compare a Singapore Permanent Secretary (S$1M+ ≈ NT$24M) to a Taiwan 常務次長 (NT$2.2M) purely on money. In institutional power, statutory oversight, and departmental command, they occupy the exact same apex civil service seat.
3. **From Semiconductor Giants to Banking: Use Organizational Seniority.**  
   ASML JG10 (Department Manager) and TSMC Grade 36 (部經理) govern complex engineering roadmaps equivalent to a Banking VP (UOB FVP / OCBC VP Senior IC). TSMC Grade 50 (副總經理 VP) and ASML JG14/15 (VP/SVP) shoulder multi-billion dollar stakes equivalent to Bank Group Managing Directors (GMD) and C-Suite Chiefs.
