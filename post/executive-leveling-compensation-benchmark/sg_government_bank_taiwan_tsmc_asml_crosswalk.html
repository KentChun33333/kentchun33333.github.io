Verified Institutional Leveling Rules (2026 Calibration):
1. TSMC VP ≡ OCBC GMD ≡ UOB Top Senior MD: At TSMC, a Vice President (G38/39 副總裁) reports to the CEO/Chairman, managing thousands of personnel and NT$30M–80M+ compensation. This equates to OCBC Group Managing Director (GMD) / Group CTO/COO, or UOB Top Senior MD / Group ExCo, rather than middle-management bank VPs.
2. Bank Ladders (OCBC vs. UOB):
• OCBC: Officer/Analyst → AM → AVP (S$120–190k) → VP (S$170–300k) → ED (S$260–380k) → MD (S$350–520k+) → MD1 (Cluster Head) → GMD (Group Managing Director / CTO / COO).
• UOB: Analyst → AM → AVP1 → AVP2 → VP1 → VP2 → FVP → SVP → ED → MD → Top Senior MD.
• OCBC AVP spans UOB AVP2 to VP1. OCBC VP (S$200k–300k core) aligns financially and organizationally with MX9 (Entry Superscale) and UOB FVP/SVP.
3. Three-Axis Methodology: (A) Within Singapore (Gov, OCBC, UOB), use compensation windows. (B) SG Gov to Taiwan Gov, use organizational rank and statutory scope. (C) ASML job grade matches TSMC job grade first, which then anchors bank grade titles.
