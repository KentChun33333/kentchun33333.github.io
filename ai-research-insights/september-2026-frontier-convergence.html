CRITICAL BOUNDARY CONDITIONS

Do Not Over-Interpret The Leap

01
"Search Compression" vs Genuine Novel Discovery Academic research shows RLVR primarily compresses search within pre-trained weights rather than creating new foundational science.
02
Verifier Dependency Ceiling RLVR excels only when programmatic ground truth exists (code, math). Open-ended subjective reasoning still suffers from subtle drift.
03
Inference Energy & Latency Cost Spending 256,000 thinking tokens scales reasoning quality but drastically increases time-to-first-token and operational power draw.
04
Synthetic Data Self-Contamination Relying exclusively on synthetic teacher distillation risks mode collapse if human ground-truth corner cases are omitted.
“The models didn’t suddenly become conscious or invent new physics. Labs simply replaced the subjective human bottleneck with programmatic compilers and granted models the compute budget to think before speaking.”

Any real-world engineering team evaluating these September 2026 frontier models should establish leak-free rolling-origin backtests, enforce hard invariant checking on generated code, and balance thinking token budgets against latency SLAs.
