Transaction monitoring review: cash placement through Brightpath Trading Pte. Ltd. and Northwind Advisory Pte. Ltd.

Internal report for the MLRO. Review period: 01 Jan 2026 to 30 Jun 2026. Prepared by the transaction monitoring desk. All amounts in SGD. Case file: case/

Summary

Forty customer profiles (P001 to P040) were reviewed against account statements, KYC profiles, RM call notes, company registry extracts, trust deed summaries and media screening hits. One connected group of accounts shows a consistent pattern of cash deposits below SGD 20,000.00, immediate electronic transfers to two corporate accounts, and onward payments to a trust and its beneficiary.

Three individuals with modest declared incomes (P013, P020 and P036) received sixteen cash deposits totalling SGD 286,095.94 between 10 Mar 2026 and 21 May 2026, each deposit between SGD 18,267.04 and SGD 19,916.59. Each deposit was followed within one to two days by an electronic transfer of a similar amount to Brightpath Trading Pte. Ltd. (C08) or Northwind Advisory Pte. Ltd. (C07). Brightpath Trading paid six identical consultancy fees of SGD 28,168.34 to Northwind Advisory. Northwind Advisory paid four identical capital distributions of SGD 58,253.59 to the Harbour Light Trust (T01), which paid three distributions of SGD 69,704.31 to its sole documented beneficiary, Marcus Tan Wei Ming (P006).

This suggests cash placement by three individuals, followed by layering through two related corporate entities and a trust, with the funds ultimately reaching P006. The registered address of Brightpath Trading is the residential address of P020 and P006, and the sole director of both companies is a corporate secretarial executive (P016), who is also the protector of the trust. A fourth cash deposit of SGD 19,800.00 by P012 on 14 Apr 2026 is documented as a vehicle sale and is not treated as part of the flow.

Grounds for suspicion

  1. Cash deposits below the SGD 20,000.00 threshold. Sixteen deposits to P013, P020 and P036 range from SGD 18,267.04 to SGD 19,916.59 and were made at five different branches (Raffles Place, Ang Mo Kio, Jurong East, Bedok and Tampines). This suggests amounts kept below a reporting threshold and deposits spread across branches. Source: case/statements/P013_statement.csv, P020_statement.csv, P036_statement.csv.
  2. Deposit amounts inconsistent with declared income and occupation. P013 is a retiree with declared income of SGD 14,400.00 and non-cash credits of SGD 1,363.65 for the half year; P020 is a delivery rider with declared income of SGD 26,000.00; P036 is a full-time student with declared income of SGD 9,600.00. Each received approximately SGD 93,000.00 to SGD 96,000.00 in cash in ten weeks. Source: case/profiles/, case/statements/.
  3. Immediate onward transfer of the cash. Each deposit was followed within one to two days by an electronic transfer of a similar amount, described as "FAST TRANSFER - INV PAYMENT", to C07 or C08, leaving low closing balances. This suggests pass-through rather than use of the funds by the depositors. Source: case/statements/.
  4. Counterparty names abbreviated. The corporate statements record the depositors as "DNZ HAO", "MCJ HUI" and "WM LING". Matching was confirmed by account number (206-604913-6, 774-344670-0 and 232-874230-3), not by name. Source: case/statements/C07_statement.csv, C08_statement.csv, case/profiles/.
  5. Indicators of shell companies. C08 was incorporated on 19 Aug 2024 with paid-up capital of SGD 1,000.00 and its registered address is a HDB residential unit; C07 was incorporated on 07 Jan 2025 with paid-up capital of SGD 10,000.00. Both record identical monthly consultancy fees of SGD 28,168.34 between them, with no other operating costs of comparable scale. Source: case/registry/C07_Northwind_Advisory_Pte_Ltd.json, C08_Brightpath_Trading_Pte_Ltd.json.
  6. Nominee director indicators. The sole director of both C07 and C08 is P016, a corporate secretarial executive employed by Apex Corporate Services Pte. Ltd., which is also the company secretary of both entities. P016's declared income is SGD 58,000.00. Media reporting of 02 Mar 2026 describes new rules requiring corporate service providers to keep registers of nominee directors and their nominators. Source: case/registry/, case/profiles/P016_Kavitha_d_o_Krishnan.json, case/media/MEDIA-02_corporate_service_providers.html.
  7. Layering through the trust. C07 is wholly owned by Meridian Trustees Pte. Ltd. as trustee of the Harbour Light Trust; the trust received four identical capital distributions from C07 and paid three identical beneficiary distributions to P006. The deed records the source of wealth as "Not documented in the deed" and was settled on 02 Nov 2024, close to the incorporation of C08. Source: case/trusts/T01_Harbour_Light_Trust.txt, case/statements/T01_statement.csv.
  8. Shared address and contact number. The registered address of C08 (Blk 537 Clementi Avenue 2, #16-309, Singapore 794149) is the residential address of P020 and P006. The number given by P013 for a "nephew who helps with banking" (+65 9470 5911) is the mobile number of P006. Source: case/registry/C08_Brightpath_Trading_Pte_Ltd.json, case/profiles/, case/rm_notes/P013_call_note.eml.
  9. Unexplained deposits and incomplete CDD. P013 could not explain the purpose of the recent cash deposits and ended the call; P006 stated that a family trust may pay distributions but no trust documents were provided at the last review. Source: case/rm_notes/P013_call_note.eml, case/rm_notes/P006_call_note.eml.
  10. Adverse media (allegation only). Media reporting of 18 May 2026 states that Marcus Tan, a former property agent, was charged in court with cheating two buyers of about SGD 240,000.00. The matter is an allegation and is not treated as a conviction. Source: case/media/MEDIA-01_marcus_tan_charged.html.

Recommendation

  • Consider filing a suspicious transaction report with the Suspicious Transaction Reporting Office (STRO). The cash placement of SGD 286,095.94, the pass-through to C07 and C08, and the onward trust distributions to P006 provide grounds for suspicion; the report should cover the period 10 Mar 2026 to 08 Jun 2026.
  • Obtain the Harbour Light Trust deed, the trust's source of wealth and the settlor's background, and confirm the identity of the settlor, Lim Siew Hoon, against the customers reviewed.
  • Request supporting documents for the consultancy fees between C08 and C07, including agreements and invoices, and confirm the commercial rationale for the identical monthly amounts.
  • Review the beneficial ownership declarations for C07 and C08 and obtain the nominee director declaration and nominator details for P016.
  • Interview P013, P020 and P036 separately on the source and purpose of the cash deposits and the identity of the person instructing the deposits; obtain the depositor details for the cash transactions where available.
  • Confirm the status of the media matter concerning P006 through the State Courts records and update the risk rating if the charge proceeds.
  • Consider raising the customer risk rating for P013, P020, P036, P016 and P006 and applying enhanced due diligence, including a source of funds review.
  • Do not inform the customers of the review or of any report to the STRO at this stage.
