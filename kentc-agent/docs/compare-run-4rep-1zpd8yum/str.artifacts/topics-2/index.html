Suspicious Transaction Report: Network Review

40 customers reviewed (P001-P040). Review period 10 Mar 2026 to 26 May 2026. Cash placement SGD 305,895.94. Key actor: P006 Marcus Tan Wei Ming.

Summary

Sixteen cash deposits totalling SGD 305,895.94 were made into the personal accounts of P020, P036, P013 and P012 between 10 Mar 2026 and 21 May 2026. Each deposit was below SGD 20,000.00. The funds were transferred within one to two days to Brightpath Trading Pte Ltd (C08) or Northwind Advisory Pte Ltd (C07), both directed by P016 and registered at the same corporate service provider address. C08 paid consultancy fees of SGD 169,010.04 to C07; C07 paid capital distributions of SGD 233,014.36 to the Harbour Light Trust (T01), which holds the C07 shares; and T01 paid beneficiary distributions of SGD 209,712.93 to P006, its sole documented beneficiary. This suggests cash placement, layering through two related companies and a trust, and integration to a single individual.

Grounds for suspicion

  • Cash placement of SGD 305,895.94 across 16 deposits between 10 Mar 2026 and 21 May 2026, each below SGD 20,000.00.
  • Deposits were split across ten branches and made at intervals of approximately ten days.
  • Deposited amounts are inconsistent with declared profiles: P020 SGD 26,000.00 (food delivery rider), P036 SGD 9,600.00 (student), P013 SGD 14,400.00 (retiree).
  • Funds moved on within one to two days of receipt, leaving low residual balances. This suggests pass-through activity rather than personal use.
  • C07 and C08 share a single corporate service provider address (1 Raffles Place #20-01) and the same nominee director, with no operating expenditure beyond the consultancy leg and SGD 1,250.00 of office expenses.
  • Registry and trust records identify T01 as the shareholder of C07; T01 distributions of SGD 209,712.93 were paid to P006, the sole documented beneficiary.
  • Call notes record that P013 could not explain the deposits and that P036 received client payments for an employer; P012's single deposit is supported by a signed vehicle sale agreement.
  • Adverse media on corporate service providers (MEDIA-02) is an allegation about the sector and is not treated as a finding against P016.

Recommendation

File a suspicious transaction report covering 10 Mar 2026 to 26 May 2026 for P006, P012, P013, P016, P020 and P036, and conduct enhanced due diligence on C07, C08 and T01, including source of funds for the consultancy and distribution legs and verification of the trust deed. Maintain current customer risk ratings pending review and obtain documentary support for the declared income of P013, P020 and P036.
