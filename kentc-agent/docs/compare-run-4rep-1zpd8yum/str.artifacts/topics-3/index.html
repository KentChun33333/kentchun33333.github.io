Grounds for suspicion

  • Fifteen cash deposits by three individuals with modest declared incomes, each between SGD 18,267.04 and SGD 19,916.59, i.e. below the SGD 20,000.00 cash reporting threshold, totalling SGD 286,095.94 (facts).
  • Each deposit was transferred out in full within one day to C07 or C08 under the label INV PAYMENT, with only SGD 2,528.00 retained across the three accounts; the placement accounts carry no other material credits (facts).
  • C07 and C08 are recently incorporated, low paid-up capital companies sharing a sole director (P016) and a corporate secretary; C07 is wholly owned by T01 (facts).
  • C07 received SGD 264,789.04 in the statement period and paid out SGD 233,014.36 to T01 with no operating costs; C08 paid only SGD 1,250.00 of office expenses, consistent with pass-through activity rather than trading (facts and inference).
  • T01, settled 02 Nov 2024 with source of wealth not documented, received the C07 distributions and paid three equal sums to its beneficiary, whose NRIC matches P006 (facts).
  • P006 received SGD 209,712.93 in two months against declared annual income of SGD 96,000.00, and applied SGD 139,808.62 to a property down payment (facts).
  • P016's declared flat fee per directorship and employment by the common corporate secretary suggest a nominee role; this suggests the companies operate under a single controlling interest (inference).
  • P013 could not explain the cash deposits when contacted; P036 said the part-time employer (C08) asked the customer to receive and forward client payments (facts from RM notes).
  • The alternate contact number for P013 (+65 9470 5911) is the mobile number on P006's profile; this suggests a connection between P013 and P006, but neither record confirms the identity of the number holder (inference).
  • C08's registered address is the residential address on P006's profile (facts).
  • Adverse media MEDIA-01 (18 May 2026) reports a Marcus Tan charged with cheating in relation to property buyers and about SGD 240,000; the reported age does not match P006's date of birth, so the match is unconfirmed and the report remains an allegation (facts and inference).
  • MEDIA-02 (02 Mar 2026) describes regulatory attention to nominee directors and shell companies receiving pass-through funds, consistent with the pattern observed (context).

Recommendation

The transaction pattern — structured cash placement by three individuals, onward same-day transfers to two related companies, round-sum consultancy fees between them, capital distributions to a trust with undocumented source of wealth, and equal beneficiary distributions to a single individual — meets the threshold for a suspicious transaction report. Recommended actions: enhanced due diligence on C07, C08, T01 and P006; obtain the trust deed, source-of-wealth evidence and the commercial rationale for the consultancy fees; identify the holder of the alternate contact number for P013; clarify the relationship between P006 and the media subject; and consider restricting cash deposits pending review. The customer should not be informed of the review or of any report filed.
