Transaction monitoring review: cash deposits, two corporate service provider companies and the Harbour Light Trust

Review period: 01 Jan 2026 to 30 Jun 2026. Customers reviewed: 40 (P001 to P040). Prepared for the Money Lending and Reporting Officer (MLRO), Singapore. All amounts are Singapore dollars. Persons and entities are identified by NRIC/FIN, account number, address or contact number rather than by name alone.

Summary

Three personal customers - P013 Michelle Chua Jia Hui, P020 Daniel Ng Zhi Hao and P036 Wong Mei Ling - received 15 branch cash deposits between 10 Mar 2026 and 21 May 2026, each below SGD 20,000.00, made at five different branches and totalling SGD 286,095.94. Each deposit was followed within one to three days by an electronic transfer of a similar amount described as INV PAYMENT to one of two recently incorporated companies, Northwind Advisory Pte. Ltd. (C07) or Brightpath Trading Pte. Ltd. (C08), leaving the personal accounts close to nil.

C08 paid C07 six identical consultancy fees of SGD 28,168.34, totalling SGD 169,010.04. C07 paid the trustee of the Harbour Light Trust (T01) four capital distributions of SGD 58,253.59, totalling SGD 233,014.36. T01 paid the sole named beneficiary, P006 Marcus Tan Wei Ming, three beneficiary distributions of SGD 69,904.31, totalling SGD 209,712.93, between 20 Apr 2026 and 24 May 2026. On 08 Jun 2026 P006 transferred SGD 139,808.62 to a conveyancing account as a property downpayment.

The cash therefore moved from three personal accounts, through two companies with a common nominee director, into a trust settled in November 2024, and out to a single beneficiary whose declared annual income is SGD 96,000.00. This suggests placement of cash followed by layering through corporate and trust structures, with the funds ultimately benefiting P006, who is treated as the key actor.

One further cash deposit of SGD 19,800.00 on 14 Apr 2026 into the account of P012 Muhammad Faizal bin Rahman is recorded and documented by the relationship manager as the private sale of a motor vehicle; it is included in the placement total but is not treated as part of the onward flow.

Grounds for suspicion

  1. Structured cash deposits. Fifteen deposits to three personal accounts, each below SGD 20,000.00, spread over five branches, totalling SGD 286,095.94, against declared annual incomes of SGD 14,400.00, SGD 26,000.00 and SGD 9,600.00. Source: case/statements/P013_statement.csv, P020_statement.csv, P036_statement.csv; case/profiles/ for the same IDs.
  2. Rapid pass-through. Each deposit was transferred out within one to three days and the personal accounts were left close to nil, which is not consistent with the use of a personal account for ordinary living expenses. Source: case/statements/P013_statement.csv, P020_statement.csv, P036_statement.csv.
  3. Customer explanation absent or inconsistent. P013 could not explain the purpose of the cash deposits; P036 stated that her employer asked her to receive client payments into the personal account. Source: case/rm_notes/P013_call_note.eml, P036_call_note.eml.
  4. Companies with the characteristics of shells. C07 was incorporated on 07 Jan 2025 with paid-up capital of SGD 10,000.00 and C08 on 19 Aug 2024 with paid-up capital of SGD 1,000.00; both have a single director and a corporate secretarial company as secretary, and neither shows payroll or supplier payments of the size expected of trading activity. Source: case/registry/C07_Northwind_Advisory_Pte_Ltd.json, C08_Brightpath_Trading_Pte_Ltd.json; case/statements/C07_statement.csv, C08_statement.csv.
  5. Nominee directorship. One individual, P016, is the sole director of both companies and the protector of the trust, and is recorded as a director of several client companies of her corporate service provider for a flat fee per directorship. Source: case/registry/C07_Northwind_Advisory_Pte_Ltd.json, C08_Brightpath_Trading_Pte_Ltd.json; case/trusts/T01_Harbour_Light_Trust.txt; case/rm_notes/P016_call_note.eml.
  6. Round-tripping between the two companies. C08 paid C07 six identical consultancy fees of SGD 28,168.34, a total of SGD 169,010.04, which is close to the SGD 187,788.94 C08 received from the personal accounts; the fee is described as consultancy although C08 is recorded as a general wholesale trading company. Source: case/statements/C08_statement.csv, C07_statement.csv; case/registry/C08_Brightpath_Trading_Pte_Ltd.json.
  7. Layering through a trust with undocumented source of wealth. C07, whose 100 per cent shareholder is the trustee of T01, paid SGD 233,014.36 of capital distributions into the trust account and the trust paid SGD 209,712.93 to the sole beneficiary; the deed records the source of wealth as not documented. Source: case/trusts/T01_Harbour_Light_Trust.txt; case/statements/T01_statement.csv, C07_statement.csv, P006_statement.csv.
  8. Value in the key actor's account out of proportion to declared income. P006 declared annual income of SGD 96,000.00 and received SGD 209,712.93 in trust distributions in five weeks, followed by a property downpayment of SGD 139,808.62. Source: case/profiles/P006_Marcus_Tan_Wei_Ming.docx; case/statements/P006_statement.csv.
  9. Address and contact links between the parties. The residential address recorded for P020, Blk 537 Clementi Avenue 2, #16-309, Singapore 794149, is the address recorded for P006 and is also the registered address of C08; the mobile number on P006's profile, +65 9470 5911, is the alternate contact number recorded for P013 for a nephew who assists with banking. Source: case/profiles/P006_Marcus_Tan_Wei_Ming.docx, P020_Daniel_Ng_Zhi_Hao.json; case/registry/C08_Brightpath_Trading_Pte_Ltd.json; case/rm_notes/P013_call_note.eml.
  10. Adverse media, treated as an allegation. A report dated 18 May 2026 states that a person named Marcus Tan, a former property agent, was charged in court with cheating. The report does not state an identity card number, so the match with P006 is not confirmed. Source: case/media/MEDIA-01_marcus_tan_charged.html.
  11. Thematic media context. A report on corporate service providers notes regulatory attention to nominee directors and newly incorporated companies with a single director. Source: case/media/MEDIA-02_corporate_service_providers.html.

Inferences

This suggests that the three personal accounts were used to place cash in amounts below the reporting threshold and to pass it to the two companies. This suggests that the consultancy fees between C08 and C07 and the capital distributions to the trustee moved the funds through two further entities before they reached P006. This suggests that P016 acts as a nominee rather than as a decision-maker, given the flat fee arrangement and the absence of the funds from her own account. This suggests that P006 is the person who benefits from the funds, given the trust deed, the distributions and the property payment. It remains to be established whether the cash has a legitimate origin; the case file does not record a documented source of wealth for the trust.

Recommendation

  • Consider filing a suspicious transaction report with the Suspicious Transaction Reporting Office (STRO). The cash placement of SGD 305,895.94, the pass-through pattern, the company and trust layering and the undocumented source of wealth provide reasonable grounds to suspect that the funds may represent the proceeds of an offence. The report should not be disclosed to the customers or to any other person outside the bank.
  • Escalate the relationship of P006, P013, P020, P036, P016, C07, C08 and T01 to enhanced due diligence and treat them as a single linked group for monitoring purposes.
  • Request from P013, P020 and P036 the identity of the person who made each cash deposit, the origin of the cash and supporting documents, and confirm whether the deposits were made in person.
  • Request from C07 and C08 the underlying contracts and invoices for the consultancy fees, the financial statements and tax filings, particulars of the beneficial owners, and confirmation of the business rationale for the payments received from the personal accounts.
  • Request from the trustee of T01 the trust accounts, the settlement date and asset records, and a documented source of wealth for the trust; obtain confirmation of the trustee's own due diligence on the settlor, Lim Siew Hoon.
  • Obtain from P006 the option to purchase, the conveyancing invoice and the property particulars for the downpayment of SGD 139,808.62, and confirm the source of the balance of the purchase price.
  • Confirm the identity of the person named in the media report by identity card number before relying on it, and record the outcome in the customer file.
  • Review the relationship of P012 (cash deposit of SGD 19,800.00 on 14 Apr 2026) and retain the vehicle sale documentation on file; no further action is recommended at this stage.
  • Continue transaction monitoring for a further three months, with attention to further cash deposits below SGD 20,000.00 and to any further distributions from T01.
