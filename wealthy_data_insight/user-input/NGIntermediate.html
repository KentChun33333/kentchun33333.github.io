Readiness Signals

These are the indicators that a family member is "ready" to be approached as a client. Each signal adds a weighted point to their overall Readiness Score (0–100). The system ranks prospects by this score.

Age in "Ready" Window (25–45)

Old enough for independent wealth decisions, young enough for long relationship horizon

Holds C-Suite or Board Role in Family Business

CEO, CFO, CTO, MD, Board Director, or VP in a family-owned entity

Named Trust Beneficiary with Distribution Rights

Has legal right to receive trust distributions (not just contingent interest)

Elite Education (Top 50 Global / Finance / Law)

MBA, JD, CFA, or degree from Ivy League / Oxbridge / IIM / NUS / LSE / etc.

Owns or Co-Owns a Registered Business

Has their own company or startup separate from family entities

Recent Property Acquisition

Bought real estate in the last 24 months (signals independent wealth)

Co-Signatory on Family Bank Accounts

Already has banking access to family funds (warmest signal)

Recent Capital Contribution to Family Entity

Invested personal funds into a family business or holding company

Active Professional / Social Presence

Active LinkedIn, speaker at industry events, or publishes content

Analytics Enrichments

Select the visualizations to generate alongside your next-generation pipeline. Each provides a different lens on the identified prospects.

Pipeline by Age Cohort

Horizontal bar chart showing the number of next-gen prospects in each age bracket. Helps prioritize outreach timing — the 25–35 band typically has the highest conversion readiness.

Relationship Network Map

Network graph showing where next-gen prospects sit relative to current clients through all selected relationship types. Color-coded by generation and readiness score. Edge style indicates relationship confidence.

Readiness Radar Chart

Multi-axis radar showing each prospect's score across all readiness dimensions. The fuller the shape, the more "ready" the prospect is to be approached.

Conversion Funnel

Shows the pipeline from identified next-gen prospects through to converted clients. Highlights where prospects drop off and where RM effort is needed.

AUM Inheritance Projection

Projected wealth transfer timeline showing when AUM is expected to shift to next-gen. Three scenarios based on current client age and estate planning signals.

Engagement Bridge Map

Shows the path from current client ecosystem to next-gen circle. Identifies "bridge" contacts (RMs, family advisors, trust officers) who can facilitate the introduction.
