System design · responsibilities · boundaries

Evidence in.
Guardrails first.
Decisions out.

Billionary Trader is a read-first decision system for account review, position risk, research, and recommendation generation. Its core promise is traceable evidence—not autonomous execution.

  • no unsupported assumptions
  • periodic data cleanup
  • account risk first
  • stale data = wait
  • execution stays separate

Three design pillars

01 Continuous self-learningReplay misses, measure drift, and reduce exposure when quality decays.
02 Walk-forward validationGround directional views in out-of-sample evidence and regime context.
03 Deterministic guardrailsData freshness, account risk, and human review can stop the flow.

03 · Safety boundaries

Review is not execution

The system can collect evidence, assess risk, and prepare advice. A recommendation does not grant authority to unlock or submit broker orders.

1. Review

Read-only evidence collection and analysis.

  • Account, positions, orders, quotes
  • Data quality and freshness
  • Risk, lessons, and playbook evidence

2. Ticket generation

A structured, reviewable action proposal.

  • Action and rationale
  • Invalidation and risk budget
  • Evidence gaps and no-trade reasons

3. Execution

A separate phase requiring explicit authority.

  • No implied broker unlock
  • No order from analysis alone
  • Human review remains the boundary
