About Kent Chiu

Pioneering algorithmic systems, autonomous multi-agent orchestration, and frontier LLM reasoning.

Kent Chiu

AI & Quant Systems Architect
MIT-CSAIL Alum Multi-Agent Systems Algorithmic Trading RLVR / GRPO

I am Kent Chiu. Autumn Memo was founded in 2016 during my research exchange in Prof. Randall Davis's Laboratory at MIT-CSAIL. What started as algorithmic inquiry into machine intelligence and mathematical models has evolved into an engineering nexus spanning autonomous multi-agent systems, test-time compute scaling, and quantitative finance.

Today, this platform serves as an evidence-grounded research workshop and production-ready demo hub. Every research study published here is accompanied by reproducible test contracts, interactive decision frontier simulators, or live offline-capable web deliverables.
