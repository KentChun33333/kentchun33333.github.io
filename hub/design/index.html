Memory has different boundaries

Public knowledge memory: the durable catalogue, source fingerprints, and curated connections implemented here.

Personal visitor memory: saved collections and cross-device reading progress would require authenticated storage. They are not silently stored in this public catalogue.

Private operating memory: client notes, tasks, proposals, and payment entitlements belong in a separate authenticated backend. They must never be committed to this public repository.

Commercial assets: selected engagements accept inquiries. Direct sales require approved price, license, delivery terms, and checkout; paid files require protected storage.
